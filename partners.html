The Problem

Office moves go wrong at the desk level

Your team handles logistics brilliantly — furniture, boxes, building access. But reconnecting IT equipment is a different skill set, and when it goes wrong, your client's first day in their new office is a disaster.

This is the gap we close.

  • 🖥️

    Monitors with no image — HDMI plugged into port 2, but displays default to port 1. Easy to miss, expensive in wasted time.

  • 💥

    Damaged ports and docking stations — cables routed too tight create tension that cracks display ports and breaks USB-C docks.

  • 🔀

    Wrong connections throughout — peripherals plugged into the wrong docking stations, displays on the wrong inputs, peripherals not working.

  • 📵

    No one to blame but you — your client doesn't know who connected what. If it doesn't work, the move company gets the call.

What We Test

Every desk. Every connection.

Standard workstation setup: dual monitors on arms, docking station, keyboard, mouse.

🖥️

Monitor Inputs

Both displays tested on the correct HDMI or DisplayPort inputs. Resolution and refresh rate confirmed.

🔌

Docking Station

Power delivery, USB hub, display output, and network passthrough all verified. Replacement fitted if damaged.

🖱️

Peripherals

Keyboard and mouse confirmed working — correct desk, correct pairing, correct USB receiver.

📐

Cable Management

All cables routed cleanly. No tension on ports. Monitor arms move freely without pulling connections.

🔍

Port Condition

Physical inspection of display ports, USB-C, and docking ports for damage caused during transit or reconnection.

✅

Full Desk Sign-Off

Each desk logged as passed or flagged. Any hardware replaced on-site is documented with cost for your records.

📋
